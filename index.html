About Me

My name is Christian Rasmussen,

and I am a

Software Engineer
Game Developer
Game Designer
Interaction Designer

.

I create interactive experiences where digital and physical worlds meet, often through Unity, LEGO SPIKE, and Raspberry Pi.

What I work on

Mixed reality simulations for learning, playful robotics workshops, and game-like interactions that make technology feel approachable.

Who it's for

Kids, learners, and people who explore best by doing, especially in educational or creative settings.

What drives me

The moments where curiosity turns into creation. Whether it's a project of my own or the first robot built by a student of mine, the goal is the same: experiencing the spark of imagination.
