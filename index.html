Welcome to My Portfolio

I'm a designer and developer who's passionate about building interactive experiences that connect play, learning, and technology. Here, you'll find a collection of projects that explore how creativity and code can bring ideas to life - from mixed reality simulations to playful robotics.

About Me

My name is Christian Rasmussen,

and I am a

Software Engineer
Game Developer
Game Designer
Interaction Designer

.

I create interactive experiences where digital and physical worlds meet - often through Unity, LEGO SPIKE and Raspberry Pi Pico. My work spans from developing mixed reality simulations for education to designing playful robotics workshops that make technology accessible and fun for children.

What drives me is the joy of learning through play - the moment when curiosity turns into creation. Whether I'm coding an AR prototype, teaching kids to program their first robot, or crafting a digital interaction, my goal is always the same: to spark imagination through meaningful, hands-on design.
